FAQ

Common questions

Who is WorkSquare for?

Employers with large shift-based or support workforces, such as construction firms and colleges, and the workers they employ. The same engine can extend to hospitals and factories.

How is overtime calculated?

Time beyond 8 hours in a day is overtime, paid at 1.5 times the hourly rate. These rules are configurable in the application.

Which languages are supported?

English and Tamil today, with the interface built so more languages can be added.

Can workers report problems anonymously?

Not yet. Reports are linked to the worker’s account so that cases can be followed up. We do not claim anonymity we cannot guarantee.

Is this production software?

WorkSquare is a prototype built for Prompt Wars 2026. The data you see is sample data, and storage resets when the service restarts.
